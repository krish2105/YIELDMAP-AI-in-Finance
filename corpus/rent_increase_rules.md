# Rent increases on renewal in Dubai

## The mechanism

A landlord in Dubai cannot raise the rent on renewal by an arbitrary amount. The permitted increase
is determined by how far the current rent sits below the market rate for comparable property, as
measured by RERA's rental index, and increases step up in bands as that gap widens. Where the
current rent is close to the market rate, no increase is permitted at all.

The RERA rental index calculator on the Dubai Land Department website is the authoritative tool for
a specific property. It takes the area, property type and current rent and returns the permitted
increase for that tenancy.

## Notice

A landlord intending to change any term of a tenancy on renewal, including the rent, must give the
tenant written notice in advance of the renewal date. Notice given late does not take effect for
that renewal.

## What this means in practice

- The question "can my landlord raise my rent by fifteen percent" cannot be answered from the
  percentage alone. It depends on how far the current rent is below the market rate for comparable
  property.
- A tenant paying close to the market rate is generally protected from an increase.
- A tenant paying well below the market rate may face a permitted increase in bands.
- Disputes go to the Rental Disputes Centre.
